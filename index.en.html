02 · PROBLEM

Two streams, three questions, one AI score

sham: a real clip whose audio is the same person's real audio from another clip, so it is out of sync with no AI involved. Lip-sync: a generator redraws the lips to match a given audio clip. A cloned voice over the original lips also breaks sync. Faces and matrices are illustrative only.

03 · DATASET

Six cells separate "AI-made" from "out of sync"

Each cell 2–6 has a full version (fake over the whole clip) and a partial one (exactly one 0.3–3 s segment). The "other clip" and the voice sample always come from the same person and the same split.

04 · REAL-DATA PIPELINE

Real-data pipeline: five steps, one model each

Model chip color: blue = visual · orange = audio · green = audio-visual sync. The numbers inside each layer are the thresholds in use. Every step runs on CPU and is written to the ledger.

05 · FAKE-GENERATION PIPELINE

Architecture follows the original SSL_Anti-spoofing, reimplemented in PyTorch. The paper EER is measured on ASVspoof 2021, under conditions unlike our data, so it is not directly comparable.

15 · RESULTS

M1 is best: AUC 0.947

Task "AI?" · test: 2,336 samples from 10 unseen people · 1 seed

AUC: probability that the model ranks a fake clip above a real one; 0.5 is guessing. 95% CI by bootstrap over people.

APPENDIX

At a 1% false-alarm threshold, M1 catches 79% of fakes

Threshold chosen on real clips of the validation set. On test, different people shift it: M1 set to 1% becomes 2.9%. Per-person calibration is needed before real use.
